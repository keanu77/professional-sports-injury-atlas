  Cycling Weekly abandon: https://www.cyclingweekly.com/news/racing/tour-de-france/romain-bardet-abandons-tour-de-france-with-concussion-467896

Initial scan “no lesion” (Bouvat, via franceinfo); **next day** Bardet said MRI showed a **“small haemorrhage”** after concussion (Instagram via Cycling Weekly / Reuters).
