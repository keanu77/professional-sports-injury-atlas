Preparing a sports medicine teaching session often means moving between a video, an event report, and follow-up information. Sports Injury Video Atlas brings those entry points into a searchable catalog.

The site indexes 200 videos across 30 sports and 14 body regions. Its 7 core cases and 193 observation pages have different levels of detail and review. The interface is primarily Traditional Chinese, with search support for English athlete names.

For developers, the repository shows how an Astro and TypeScript application can separate review output from public release, track source provenance, and validate artifacts. Production uses Cloudflare Pages, Functions, and D1. The new fork guide includes an isolated fictional demo for experimenting with your own title and content without packaging the medical collection or third-party media.

We welcome focused contributions: clearer setup instructions, accessibility improvements, reproducible software reports, and dated source corrections. The guide also identifies where local development stops and independent rights review, clinical review, and publishing authorization begin.

Original code and applicable developer documentation are MIT-licensed. Editorial records, medical content, and media have separate rights. The atlas supports education and discussion; it does not establish diagnoses or return-to-sport clearance, and we make no teaching-outcome claim.
