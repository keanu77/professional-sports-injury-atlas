# NBA source policy (`nba-v7`)

This immutable addendum is the append-only Decision 14 named-photographer
event-photo policy. Its machine-readable companion is
`source-policy.v7.json`. It binds the exact immutable `nba-v6` JSON and
Markdown pair and the evidence-first Phase 1 report committed before this
policy. All compatible clinical, video, privacy, licensing, attribution,
notice, download, SVG, crop, fallback, and composite-use controls continue
unchanged.

Decision 14 does not authorize medical-content changes, a medical-review
decision, publication, deployment, indexing, hosting-service mutation,
external-account mutation, or remote-repository mutation. Occasion and
photographer-risk findings remain triage evidence, never copyright,
personality-rights, legal-use, medical, or public-release approval.

## Frozen evidence result

The bound Phase 1 evidence reviewed every photographer/account connected to
the complete fixed-query lead population for the 16 current SVG covers. No
reviewed photographer passed every non-waivable identity, self-publication,
numeric history, exact-license, fixed-reuse, dispute-search, and washing-signal
gate. The `nba-v7` named allowlist therefore contains exactly zero entries.
There is no minimum photo count, so an empty allowlist is a valid fail-closed
result. A later name requires new evidence and an append-only policy successor;
this version is never edited in place.

Non-allowlisted photographers remain governed by the unchanged Decision 13
non-event branch. With this version's empty allowlist, no game, pregame,
warm-up, All-Star, postseason, preseason-competition, or international-
competition photo can use the conditional Decision 14 event branch.

## Conditional event branch

Eligibility remains exactly:

```text
(complete nba-v6 qualified non-event photo)
OR
(finite factual event occasion
 AND exact named allowlist photographer/account join
 AND verified photographer-owned self-published original
 AND complete independent per-file checks
 AND exact candidate/history license-token match
 AND different injury event verified
 AND credentialed-access-residual-risk retained
 AND exact Decision 14 owner-record reference retained)
```

Every other result uses the current original SVG fallback. Merely appearing on
an allowlist would not clear a work. Commons/Flickr uploader names, display
names, copied license labels, filenames, categories, snippets, and third-party
imports cannot independently prove the photographer, account control, or
licensing authority.

The only conditional event occasions are:

- `all-star-game-action`
- `international-competition-action`
- `live-preseason-competition`
- `postseason-action`
- `regular-season-action`
- `regular-season-pregame-or-warm-up`

The v6 ineligible list remains the default; it is not deleted or globally
redefined. `ambiguous-credentialed-court-context`, `closed-practice`, `other`,
`unknown`, and `unresolved` always fail, including for a future allowlisted
photographer. A same-injury-event or unresolved injury-event relationship also
always fails.

## Named allowlist floor

Every future successor entry must bind a verified individual public identity
to exact photographer-owned Flickr or direct-own Commons account IDs and URL
boundaries. It must document at least three complete years and 50 reproducibly
enumerated self-published sports works; a license-independent,
date-stratified deterministic sample of at least 30 with 100% one exact
supported CC token and zero unexplained deviations; and a fixed
Commons/Wikipedia reuse chain spanning at least 24 complete months.

The dated bounded dispute search must locate no authorship, license, removal,
or takedown dispute under its stated method. This is bounded negative evidence,
not proof of no risk. The five separately evidenced washing checks are account
compilation/repost behavior, capture time/location plausibility, EXIF-series
consistency, native-resolution patterns, and neighboring-event sequence
continuity. Any failed, unresolved, missing, or stale required result rejects
the entry. Commons thumbnails or third-party derivatives cannot prove original
EXIF or native-resolution consistency.

## Per-file obligations

Every future event photo still requires independently opened current Commons
description and fixed revision, and the photographer-owned upstream original
for an import or derivative. Preserve athlete/case identity, original URL and
uploader account, exact filename/dimensions, supplied-title state, credited
maker and uploader relationship, exact license/version/link and bucket,
copyright/disclaimer/other notices, alteration, focal position, local bytes,
and supersession facts.

For CC BY-SA crops, the Adapter's License must link the same CC BY-SA version
and apply only to the crop contribution. CC BY-SA material remains forbidden
as an input to site-composed OG, social, case-card, infographic, or other baked
design images. Ordinary local HTML/CSS placement and ordinary responsive
renditions remain distinct and still require complete attribution.

A qualifying event photo must separately record the finite factual occasion
and `credentialed-access-residual-risk`; it must cite
`../.claude/g2-owner-decisions.md`, heading
`Decision 14 — 放寬為具名低風險攝影者的賽場封面照`. Its generic public
sentence is `賽事場合資料照片；非本案例受傷當下影像`, accompanied by an
asset-specific factual caption. This bounded owner acceptance is not a finding
of unrestricted commercial authority and must never be represented as no
risk, complete rights clearance, lawful public use, or public-release
clearance.

The fixed internal statement is: `Decision 14 accepts a bounded
credentialed-access residual risk; no copyright, personality-rights, or
public-release approval is created.` The event branch must retain that exact
non-clearance statement and the immutable Phase 1 evidence digest as separate
conjuncts; neither can be inferred from the occasion or account name.

## Protected predecessor and artifact boundaries

The five current Commons covers—CJ McCollum, Dwight Powell, Kevin Durant,
Kyrie Irving, and LeBron James—remain outside Decision 14 discovery and
promotion scope. Their complete registry subtrees, local bytes, factual
captions, and credit rows remain unchanged. The target roster is exactly the 16
current Decision 13 SVG covers. A non-event candidate may still qualify only
through unchanged v6 rules; otherwise the exact current SVG remains.

Historical `nba-v1` through `nba-v6` policy files and all historical player-
photo registries remain byte-immutable. The next registry must bind this exact
policy pair and the exact registry v5 predecessor. Same-version drift, an
unlisted photographer, a shared-account ambiguity, an uploader/account
mismatch, a third-party-only source, a missed threshold, sample drift, a
license mismatch, stale evidence, unresolved washing or injury relationship,
an unknown occasion, a missing owner citation, or affirmative-clearance
wording all fail closed.
