# NBA source policy (`nba-v6`)

This immutable addendum is the append-only Decision 13 correction to `nba-v5`. Its machine-readable companion is `source-policy.v6.json`. The exact `nba-v5` JSON and Markdown bytes remain unchanged and are bound as this version's predecessor. All clinical, diagnosis, video, provider, rights, provenance, fallback, and composition controls continue unchanged except for the three internally contradictory inherited photo fields corrected below.

This correction does not authorize medical-content changes, a medical-review decision, publication, deployment, indexing, hosting mutation, external-account mutation, or remote-repository mutation. Occasion and photographer-risk classifications remain triage evidence, not copyright, personality-rights, or public-release clearance.

## Corrected current-photo consistency

The filename `2025_C.J._McCollum_(cropped).jpg` is removed from `explicitNegativeFiles`. `nba-v5` simultaneously admitted independently licensed, verified non-event media-day photographs and retained that filename's older `official-media-day-portrait` rejection from `nba-v4`. Under this version a filename is never self-clearing: the current candidate must still pass the complete identity, fixed-revision, original-source, exact-license, occasion, photographer/uploader, residual-risk, notice, local-byte, and crop checks.

The superseded `non-basketball-court` token is removed from `prohibitedClassifications`. Decision 13 expressly admits verified press conferences, public events or fan interactions, and award ceremonies, so an accepted non-event photograph need not depict a basketball court. Game action, regular-season pregame or warm-up, live preseason competition, ambiguous credentialed-court context, official headshots, agency/newswire material, and every other `nba-v5` red line remain rejected.

The generic non-event caption is `非賽事場合資料照片；非本案例受傷當下影像`. Each photo keeps its more specific registry-authored occasion caption adjacent to the image. The generic policy wording neither replaces those asset-specific facts nor describes public-event photographs as court photographs.

## Unchanged attribution and composition controls

Every accepted photo still retains its supplied title or explicit `not-supplied` state, photographer/uploader identities, exact versioned license link, fixed Commons revision, alteration disclosure, and supplied notice state. BY-SA presentation crops retain their linked compatible Adapter's License, limited to the crop contribution. CC BY-SA material remains forbidden as an input to any site-composed OG, social, case-card, infographic, or other baked design image; ordinary local HTML/CSS placement and ordinary responsive renditions remain distinct.

Original SVG fallbacks remain site-owned and must contain no external fetch, embedded or linked raster, copied likeness or team/league mark, script, foreign object, third-party font, or third-party artwork. They receive no invented Creative Commons license.

## Immutable successor cycle

Historical `nba-v1` through `nba-v5` policy files remain byte-immutable. The current player-photo registry must advance append-only, bind the exact registry v4 predecessor bytes, bind this exact `nba-v6` policy pair, and preserve all 21 athlete / 26 case selection facts and local asset bytes. Validators reject a current file that also appears in the explicit-negative list, restoration of `non-basketball-court`, drift of the generic caption, predecessor-selection drift, policy drift, or same-version registry drift.
